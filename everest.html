Mt Everest

About Mount Everest

  • Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world, standing at an elevation of 8,848.86 meters (29,031.7 feet) above sea level. Located in the Himalayan range on the border of Nepal and Tibet, Everest is known locally as Sagarmatha in Nepal and Chomolungma in Tibet.

  • Every year, thousands of adventurers, trekkers, and climbers visit the Everest region to experience its breathtaking landscapes, unique Sherpa culture, and world-famous trekking routes. While reaching the summit is a challenge reserved for experienced mountaineers, visitors can enjoy spectacular views from places such as Namche Bazaar, Tengboche, and Everest Base Camp.
  • The Everest region is home to stunning glaciers, deep valleys, ancient monasteries, and some of the most beautiful mountain scenery in the world. It remains one of Nepal's most iconic tourist destinations and a symbol of adventure and achievement.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit the Everest region is during spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November). During these seasons, the weather is generally clear, providing excellent mountain views and ideal trekking conditions.
