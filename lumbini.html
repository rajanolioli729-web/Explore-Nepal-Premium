Lumbini

About Lumbini

Lumbini is one of the most important spiritual and historical destinations in Nepal. It is recognized as the birthplace of Lord Buddha, the founder of Buddhism, and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Every year, thousands of pilgrims, monks, and tourists from around the world visit Lumbini to experience its peaceful atmosphere and rich cultural heritage. The main attraction of Lumbini is the Maya Devi Temple, which marks the exact place where Queen Maya Devi gave birth to Siddhartha Gautama (Lord Buddha) around 563 BCE. Visitors can also explore the Sacred Garden, Ashoka Pillar, monasteries built by different countries, meditation centers, and beautiful ponds surrounding the site. Lumbini offers a unique blend of history, spirituality, and cultural significance, making it one of Nepal's most treasured destinations and an ideal place for peace, reflection, and learning.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Lumbini is from October to March when the weather is pleasant and ideal for sightseeing. The Buddha Jayanti festival is also a special time to experience the spiritual significance of Lumbini.
